Runnable browser demo with exactly three home picture tiles, mic and More; no typing in core farmer flows. Shared locale JSON, persistent query/state/photo caches, camera/blur/compression, confidence states, care/label arithmetic, picture diary/benchmark consent, land search/filter/compare/notice/interest/owner simulation, small helpers and consent-aware mock inbox.
