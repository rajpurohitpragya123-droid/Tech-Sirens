# Tech Sirens — AI-Powered Smart Agriculture & IoT Ecosystem
### Hackathon Presentation & Pitch Deck

## 1. Problem Statement
Smallholder farmers in India suffer from severe harvest losses (up to 35% annually) due to unaddressed crop diseases, erratic weather patterns, lack of affordable soil testing facilities, and exploitation by middlemen in local mandis.

## 2. Our Innovation: The Tech Sirens Ecosystem
Tech Sirens delivers an end-to-end agritech solution combining edge hardware with accessible AI:
1. **Low-Cost Solar IoT Field Node**: ESP32 microcontroller with soil moisture, pH, and environmental sensors communicating over MQTT.
2. **AI Crop Disease Diagnosis**: Mobile computer vision classifier diagnosing leaf pests and fungal infections offline in under 2 seconds.
3. **Multilingual Vernacular Voice Assistant**: Conversational AI communicating in Hindi, Marathi, and Punjabi over WhatsApp and local phone audio.
4. **Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Bazaar**: Fair-price agricultural marketplace cutting out predatory broker intermediaries.

## 3. Technology Architecture
- **Hardware/Firmware**: ESP32, FreeRTOS, PlatformIO, Capacitive Soil Sensors.
- **Backend & Cloud**: Node.js/Express, Supabase, PostgreSQL RLS, Mosquitto MQTT Broker, Python PyTorch ML pipeline.
- **Applications**: React 19 / Vite PWA, React Native / Expo Mobile App, Next.js Admin Dashboard.
